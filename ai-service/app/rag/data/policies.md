# AuraCommerce Store Policies & Customer Service Guide

## 1. Return & Refund Policy
- **Window**: Customers may request a full refund or return within **30 calendar days** of delivery.
- **Condition**: Items must be in their original packaging, unused, and include all original accessories, cables, and manuals.
- **Refund Process**: Once a return is initiated and received, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Restocking Fees**: No restocking fees are charged for defective products or store errors. A 10% fee applies to opened non-defective computer hardware.
- **Supervisor Authorization Rule**: Any automated refund exceeding **$50.00 USD** automatically triggers a human-in-the-loop review by a senior store manager before funds are disbursed.

## 2. Shipping & Delivery
- **Standard Shipping**: 3-5 business days across the contiguous United States. Free on orders over $50.00; otherwise $5.99 flat rate.
- **Express Shipping**: 1-2 business days via DHL Express for $14.99.
- **Order Processing**: Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST ship same day from our nearest distribution fulfillment center.
- **Tracking**: Real-time DHL tracking numbers are generated automatically upon carrier dispatch.

## 3. Product Warranty & Technical Support
- **Standard Warranty**: All electronics and accessories include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects.
- **Extended Protection**: AuraCare+ provides 2-year accidental damage coverage for laptops and audio gear.
- **Defective on Arrival (DOA)**: Products found non-functional within 48 hours of delivery qualify for instant expedited replacement at zero cost.

## 4. Price Match Guarantee
- We match current pre-tax prices for identical, new items sold and shipped by major authorized retailers within 14 days of purchase.
